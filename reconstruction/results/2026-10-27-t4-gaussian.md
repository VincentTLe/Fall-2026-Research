## 1. Result

The nerfstudio + gsplat + COLMAP toolchain works on the server. There was no phone video, so (per Tan) Step 1 used
nerfstudio's sample capture `poster`. COLMAP posed **98 of 100** frames. splatfacto trained 30k steps in about 10 min
using under 2 GB of GPU memory, and reaches **PSNR 35.1 / SSIM 0.967 / LPIPS 0.079** on 9 held-out frames. The
original download link for `poster` is dead. The copy used here (nerfstudio team's Hugging Face) has only 100 of the
capture's 226 frames. GaussianAvatar (Steps 2–3) is blocked: its data is in a OneDrive folder that needs a browser,
and building its CUDA code was not permitted in this session.

## 4. Numbers

**COLMAP** (`poster`, 100 frames 1080×1920, SIFT on GPU 0, exhaustive matching): mean 3730 features per image
(min 1122). 1509 image pairs had matches. The mapper built two models:

| Model | Registered images | 3D points | Mean reprojection error |
|---|---|---|---|
| `sparse/0` | 2 (frames #73, #76, a weak first pair) | 300 | 0.17 px |
| `sparse/1` | **98** | 12,651 | 0.82 px |

The two models share no frames (2 + 98 = 100). Focal length from `sparse/1`: fx 1145.7, fy 1156.2 px. The capture's
own transforms.json gives fx 1147.2, fy 1158.8, so they agree within 0.2%. COLMAP steps took about 1 min in total.

**splatfacto** (nerfstudio 1.1.5 defaults, 30,000 steps, auto downscale ×2 → 540×960, `fraction` split 0.9 → 89
train / 9 eval frames, GPU 0):

| Quantity | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Training time | 590.5 s ("Train Total"); sum of step times 468 s; 608 s wall for the whole `ns-train` command | TensorBoard, shell timer |
| Step time | ~6 ms early, 11.4 ms at the end | train log |
| Gaussians | 193,255 at the end (max 245,682 during densification) | `gaussian_count` |
| Peak GPU memory | 1822.8 MB torch max allocated; nvidia-smi peak 1374 MiB on GPU 0 (1-s samples, incl. 69 MiB Xorg) | TensorBoard, `p3-splatfacto-gpumem.csv` |
| Checkpoint | 146.7 MB | `step-000029999.ckpt` |
| Held-out, step 30k (`ns-eval`) | PSNR 35.09 ± 1.98, SSIM 0.9666 ± 0.0065, LPIPS 0.0785 ± 0.0126 | `eval-step30000.json` |
| Held-out, step 29k (in training) | PSNR 34.85, SSIM 0.9665, LPIPS 0.0784, 95.6 fps | TensorBoard |
| Train-view PSNR, last logged | 38.98 | TensorBoard |
| Video | 264 frames, 540×960, 8.9 s; rendered in 16 s including model loading | `ffprobe`, shell timer |

Render speed: the in-training eval reports 95.6 fps at 540×960. `ns-eval` reports only 2.4 fps on the same frames.
I think its timer includes more than the render, but I did not confirm that. The 264-frame video took 16 s *including*
model loading and video encoding, so pure rendering is at least ~17 fps. I would quote "about 96 fps" with this caveat.

## 5. Checks

- I looked at five frames (0, 66, 132, 198, 263) of `poster-interpolate.mp4`. The camera goes most of the way around the
  chair and poster. The poster text ("PROTECT Your Mental Health") is readable, the floor and walls are clean, and I saw
  no floaters in those frames.
- I looked at held-out frame `eval_img_0004.png` (ground truth | render side by side). It is hard to tell the two apart;
  the window highlights are slightly softer in the render.
- Numbers come from two sources that agree: the in-training eval at step 29k and `ns-eval` at step 30k (PSNR 34.85 vs.
  35.09, the same SSIM and LPIPS).
- Data integrity: every file matches the hash Hugging Face lists for that commit. `transforms.json` lists 226 frames,
  and 126 of them are missing on disk, which is how I found the frame gap.
- Peak-memory values: torch's max allocated (1823 MB) is higher than the highest nvidia-smi sample (1374 MiB). So the
  peak was a spike shorter than 1 s that the sampler missed. I report torch's number as the peak.

## 6. Failed or skipped

- **No phone video.** As Tan instructed, the nerfstudio sample capture replaced it. So `ns-process-data video` (frame
  extraction from a video) was not exercised; `ns-process-data images` ran the same COLMAP path on frames.
- **`ns-download-data nerfstudio --capture-name=poster` fails.** gdown: "Cannot retrieve the public link of the file".
  The Drive file `1FceQ5DX7bbTbHeL26t0x6ku56cwsRs6t` returns 404 even on its view page. nerfstudio issue #3716 reports the
  nerfstudio Drive datasets as dead since about June 2026; #3762 points to the HF copy. **That copy has only 100 of 226
  frames** (numbers 1–217 with gaps). All Step 1 numbers are for those 100 frames, so they are not comparable with
  published `poster` results.
- **nerfstudio picked the wrong COLMAP model.** `ns-process-data` reads only `colmap/sparse/0` and reported "COLMAP
  only found poses for 2.00% of the images". The real reconstruction was `sparse/1` (98 frames). I converted that one
  with `--colmap-model-path colmap/sparse/1`. The first `transforms.json` is kept as `transforms.sparse0.json`. For a
  real phone video, check every `sparse/N` with `colmap model_analyzer` before believing a low count.
- **`ns-render spiral` does not work with splatfacto in 1.1.5.** It first rejected `--rots/--zrate` (not options in
  this version), then failed `assert isinstance(...)` at `render.py:673`, because it needs a ray-based datamanager. The
  orbit video uses `interpolate` along the training cameras instead. A first `interpolate` with default `--pose-source eval`
  only gave a 1.2 s clip (9 eval cameras) and was deleted.
- **Steps 2 and 3 (GaussianAvatar on People-Snapshot/NeuMan, then REHAB24-6) did not run.**
  - Its data (`assets.zip`, `gs_data.zip`, `pretrained_models.zip`) is only in a SharePoint/OneDrive shared folder.
    The folder page opens (HTTP 200), but command-line access to the folder contents was blocked in this session. Tan
    needs to download it in a browser.
  - Cloning the 3DGS repo and compiling `diff-gaussian-rasterization` / `simple-knn` (required by the GaussianAvatar
    README) was refused by the session's permission check. Tan has to approve that.
  - GaussianAvatar itself is cloned at `~/fall2026/external/GaussianAvatar`, commit
    `d981c62238ef64e89dcc04719d2ebbb4758b080a` (2024-03-26). No env built, nothing run.
- Leftovers outside `runs/`/`logs/`, not deleted (RULES: ask first): the empty folders
  `~/fall2026/data/nerfstudio/nerfstudio/poster` and `.temp_poster` from the failed `ns-download-data`, and
  `~/fall2026/data/downloads/nerfstudio-poster.zip`, which is the 1652-byte Google 404 page, not a zip.
